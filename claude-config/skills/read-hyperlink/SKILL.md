# Read Hyperlink

Process any shared hyperlink (YouTube video or website URL) and return a structured response adhering strictly to these three parts:

1. **Summary:** A concise overview of the material, limited to a maximum of 3 paragraphs, capturing the core premise and context.
2. **Key Points:** A clear bulletpoint list highlighting the primary takeaways, findings, or arguments.
3. **Comprehensive Breakdown:** A full, detailed, and structured section exploring the specific topics, sections, or technical details in depth.

# Instructions

* Retrieve video metadata and transcripts for YouTube links, or fetch content for web URLs.
* Ensure the summary section strictly respects the limit of 1 to 3 paragraphs.
* Maintain a direct, clean, and objective presentation without conversational filler.
* Do not include the original link in the output.
* Do not count this as the final response. This is an interim response.
